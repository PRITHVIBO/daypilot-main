02 / LEARNING TRACKERS

Connect the browser you actually use.

Summary only
  1. Open your updated tracker and choose Connect DayPilot.
  2. Copy the tracker’s displayed origin into this form.
  3. Create a connection, then paste its URL, ID, and token into that tracker.
  4. Choose automatic sync there to send changes while the tracker is open.

Tokens expire after 90 days, are restricted to one tracker and origin, and only write aggregate counts. They cannot read your DayPilot data. Code, queries, and personal notes are not sent.
